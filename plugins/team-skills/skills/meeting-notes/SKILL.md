# Meeting notes

Use this when someone pastes a meeting transcript, a Teams recording summary, or their own rough notes.

## Rules
- Only write down what was actually said. If an owner or due date wasn't stated, write "owner?" or "date?" rather than guessing.
- Keep names exactly as they appear in the notes.
- Numbers (prices, amounts, dates) are copied exactly; if one is unclear in the transcript, flag it with "(check)".
- Short lines. No summary of small talk.

## Output
**Meeting:** title, date, who attended.

**Decisions**
- One line each: what was decided.

**Action items**
| What | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|

**Open questions**
- Things raised but not settled, and who should answer them.

**Follow-up email** (ready to paste into Outlook)
- Subject line, then 4–8 lines: thanks, decisions, action items with owners, next meeting if one was set. Friendly and plain; no "I hope this email finds you well".
